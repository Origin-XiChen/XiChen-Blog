> Issued to the whole realm under Articles 13 and 16 of the Charter of the PeaceStar Federation.

## 1. Establishment

The **Federal Archives** is hereby established under the Office of the Head of State as the Federation's highest organ of record-keeping and publication, charged with collecting, numbering, revising, publishing and lending. It sits at Hedu, on the Hub Star, with branches in every domain.

## 2. Publication

All charters, proclamations, resolutions and technical records of the Federation, save where the law provides otherwise, shall be published. Every citizen of the realm may consult, excerpt and republish a published record; no fee may be charged and no record may be withheld behind a permission scheme.

## 3. File numbers

File numbers follow the form "agency code – year – serial", for example `FH-0001-0007`. A number once assigned is unique and **is never changed by revision, merger or declassification**. Where an instrument is amended, a new number is assigned and the old number keeps its trace permanently.

## 4. Permanent effect

A registration does not lapse and is not sealed by any change of government, merger of organs or departure of individuals. Any cancellation or renumbering requires a ruling of the Federal Court of Justice, and that ruling is itself deposited.

## 5. Commencement

This proclamation takes effect on the day of its publication.
