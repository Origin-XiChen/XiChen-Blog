> Adopted at the fifth plenary session of the Supreme Assembly. Seats: 320; present: 307; in favour: 301; abstentions: 6; against: 0.

## 1. The calendar

From Stellar Year 1, First Month, First Day the whole realm reckons time by the stellar calendar. Domain calendars may be used for custom and festival, but official documents, treaties, archives and inscriptions shall use the stellar calendar exclusively.

## 2. File numbers

File numbers follow the form "agency code – year – serial": an agency code of two or three letters, then four digits for the year and four for the serial, for example `FC-0001-0003`. The year is the stellar year; a real-world year shall not be used.

## 3. Agency codes

The first registered agency codes are as follows. Codes for bodies added later shall be settled and proclaimed by the Federal Archives together with the Office of the Head of State.

- `FA` — Federal Archives
- `FC` — Supreme Assembly
- `FH` — Office of the Head of State
- `FJ` — Federal Court of Justice
- `FT` — Federal Institute of Technical Standards

## 4. Revision

The Federal Archives revises its holdings once a year. Errors are listed in that year's errata schedule and the original is not altered; the errata schedule is itself registered under a file number.

## 5. Commencement

This resolution takes effect on the day of its adoption and stands alongside Article 16 of the Charter of the PeaceStar Federation.
