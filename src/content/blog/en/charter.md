> This is the registered copy of the Federation's founding law, revised by the Federal Archives. The text agrees with the original instrument; where any discrepancy exists, the original prevails.

## Preamble

The five domains of Xihe, Panxing, Canglan, Changfeng and Guiyuan, having endured ages of division and knowing well the calamity of arms, do now by free will unite as a Federation — to keep the peace together, to share the stars together, and to bear honour and hardship together. This Charter is therefore enacted, as a pledge to be kept and handed down to later generations.

## Chapter I — General Provisions

**Article 1.** The PeaceStar Federation is a union of the five domains, entered into by free will. Sovereignty rests with all citizens of the Federation and may not be usurped by any organ, body or individual.

**Article 2.** The Federation takes "Peace above all, stars shine together" as its motto. The Federation maintains no expeditionary army; armed force is kept solely for the defence of the realm, the protection of the people and relief in disaster.

**Article 3.** The territory of the Federation is the sum of the five domains together with their star systems, orbits and installations. Any change of territory requires a decision of the Supreme Assembly and shall be made known throughout the realm by proclamation.

## Chapter II — The Federation and the Domains

**Article 4.** Each domain retains its own customs, dialects and practices of governance, provided they do not conflict with this Charter.

**Article 5.** The division of powers follows this principle: the Federation holds foreign affairs, defence, the calendar, archives and interstellar navigation; each domain holds welfare, education, industry and public order.

**Article 6.** Each domain governs itself under its own laws; no barrier may be raised against the movement of finance, people or resources between domains.

## Chapter III — Rights and Duties of Citizens

**Article 7.** The freedoms of movement, speech, belief, writing and association of every citizen of the Federation are protected by the Federation.

**Article 8.** A citizen's dwelling, correspondence and records are inviolable. No organ may open, intercept or alter them except under law.

**Article 9.** Citizens are obliged to pay taxes under law, to serve in the defence of the realm and to report truthfully; they are likewise obliged to guard the peace and to suffer no sedition.

## Chapter IV — Federal Organs

**Article 10.** The Supreme Assembly is the legislative organ of the Federation, composed of seats elected by each domain in proportion to its population and the scale of its star territory. It decides laws, budgets and changes of territory.

**Article 11.** The Office of the Head of State is the executive organ of the Federation. The Head of State is elected by the Supreme Assembly for a term of five years and may serve one further term only.

**Article 12.** The Federal Court of Justice is the judicial and constitutional organ of the Federation, exercising its authority independently and free from interference by the executive.

**Article 13.** The Federal Archives is the archival organ of the Federation, independently registering, revising and publishing federal instruments. Once registered, no entry may be sealed or altered by any change of government.

## Chapter V — Flag, Calendar and Records

**Article 14.** The national flag is a vertical bicolour of crimson and star blue, bearing at its centre a golden five-pointed star with a plain white ring of peace set within it. The making and use of the flag are prescribed separately.

**Article 15.** The year of the Federation's founding is Stellar Year 1, and the whole realm reckons time by it. Domain calendars may be kept alongside it, but official documents shall use the stellar calendar.

**Article 16.** The founding instruments, resolutions and engineering records of the Federation shall all be given a file number and deposited with the Federal Archives for the reference of later generations.

## Chapter VI — Amendment and Final Provisions

**Article 17.** Any amendment to this Charter requires the assent of two-thirds or more of all seats in the Supreme Assembly and the acceptance of more than half of the domains.

**Article 18.** This Charter takes effect on Stellar Year 1, First Month, First Day. Any earlier law conflicting with it ceases to have force from that day.
